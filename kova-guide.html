1 · What HQ is (30 seconds)

HQ is your personal command center — one prioritized view over your day, the Kova companies, real estate, finances, investments, family, health, documents, goals, and (soon) your AI agents. It is built around one idea: attention, not information. The Command Center should answer five questions within ten seconds of opening it:

What matters today? What's at risk? What needs a decision? What changed? Where should attention go next?

Everything else — the 14 other screens — exists to keep those answers honest. Healthy systems stay quiet.

2 · Full setup checklist

Do these once, in order. Badges show whose move it is: live already done · you needs your hands · next build on the roadmap.

  1. Install HQ on your phone you
    Open https://hq.aarondweymann.workers.dev in Safari (iPhone) → Share button → Add to Home Screen. It installs as a standalone app named "HQ" with its own icon. On desktop, bookmark the same URL (Chrome also offers "Install HQ" in the address bar).
  2. Choose your sync passphrase — carefully you
    Settings → Device sync. Pick a passphrase of 3–4 random words (e.g. "copper heron velvet marathon" — not that one now that it's written here). This passphrase is your data's encryption key: it never leaves your devices, the server only ever stores scrambled ciphertext, and nobody — not Cloudflare, not anyone — can recover it if lost. Write it on paper and store it where you keep passports. Enable sync with the same passphrase on phone and desktop; the second device will offer to adopt the first one's data.
  3. Replace the demo data with your reality you
    Everything on screen is editable demo data. Work through it in this order — about an hour total, and the app becomes real:
    ① Finance: edit the account list to your real balances (tap any account).
    ② Investments: edit positions to your actual holdings (tap a row).
    ③ Real Estate: correct the owned-property numbers and prune the pipeline to live deals.
    ④ Tasks / Projects / Calendar: delete what isn't yours, capture what is (c is fastest).
    ⑤ Settings → Data → Export backup when done — your first restore point.
  4. Lock the two accounts that ARE your supply chain you
    The app's code deploys from GitHub through Cloudflare. Whoever controls those accounts controls the code your browser runs — they are the real security boundary. On GitHub: Settings → Password & authentication → enable 2FA (passkey or hardware key beats SMS). On Cloudflare: My Profile → Authentication → enable 2FA. Ten minutes, biggest risk reduction available.
  5. Optional but recommended: put a login gate in front of HQ you
    Today anyone with the URL can load the app shell (they see only demo data — your data never leaves your devices). To make even the page private: bring any domain into Cloudflare (~$10/yr), attach it to the hq Worker (Workers & Pages → hq → Settings → Domains & Routes), then Zero Trust → Access → Applications → add the domain with a policy allowing only your email. After that, opening HQ asks for a one-time code to your inbox first. Free at personal scale.
  6. When the AI machine arrives: bring the agents to life you — full steps in section 7.
  7. Real market prices & brokerage import next build
    Live public price feeds, Fidelity CSV import, and the SnapTrade read-only pipe are the next builds — ask Claude to build them when you're ready. Until then, tap any position to update its price manually.

3 · The operating rhythm

Morning — 10 minutes

Open Today. Read the brief. Set (or confirm) the three outcomes — the test is: if everything else slipped but these got done, today was a win. Scan the attention feed on the Command Center; approve or dismiss anything agents queued overnight.

During the day — seconds at a time

Capture everything the moment it lands — c on desktop, the big ＋ on your phone. "pay water bill friday" becomes a dated task by itself. Never keep a commitment in your head; the inbox and capture box are the only two places things are allowed to enter.

Evening — 5 minutes

Today → Start daily shutdown. Check off outcomes, push what's left to tomorrow, write one line of notes, name tomorrow's first outcome. Closing the day deliberately is what keeps the mornings calm.

Friday — 30 minutes

Goals & Reviews → Run weekly review. The system pre-fills what it knows (completions, overdues, projects at risk, agent activity); you add judgment: wins, root causes of misses, and next week's five outcomes. This is the highest-leverage half hour of the system.

6 · Security & privacy model — the honest version

HQ was designed so that no company can read your data. Here is exactly what sits where:

PlaceWhat it holdsWho can read it
Your browser (each device)Your full data, in localStorageAnyone using your unlocked device — protect devices with passcodes/FileVault.
Cloudflare (the hq Worker)The app's code, demo seed, and your sync record — ciphertext only, AES-256 encrypted in your browser with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2, 310k rounds). Plus ordinary web logs (IPs, timestamps).Content: nobody without your passphrase. Metadata (that something synced, when, from which IP): Cloudflare.
GitHubThe app's source code and demo data only. Never your real data.Public repo — which is fine, because nothing personal is in it.
Your AI machine (soon)Model inference, n8n credentials for Gmail/Calendar/etc.Only you — that's the point of it.

What this already defeats

Data-mining by the host ✓ (ciphertext only) · a Cloudflare data breach exposing your finances ✓ (nothing readable to breach) · someone finding the URL ✓ (they get a demo) · a stolen backup of the server ✓ · cloud AI companies reading your life ✓ (the model is yours, on your hardware).

The two honest residual risks — and their fixes

1 · The supply chain. Like every web app on earth (including your bank's), the JavaScript your browser runs comes from a server. Someone who compromised your GitHub or Cloudflare account could ship altered code that reads data inside your browser, where it is necessarily decrypted. Fix: hardware-key 2FA on both accounts (setup step 4) — and, for the maximum end state, self-host HQ on your AI machine behind Tailscale so no third party serves the code at all. The app is a folder of static files; it runs anywhere.
2 · Your passphrase and your devices. The encryption is only as strong as the passphrase (use 4 words), and the app is only as private as an unlocked device. Plaintext JSON backups you export are unencrypted — store them like cash.
Where this lands: as deployed, HQ is meaningfully more private than any commercial finance/productivity app — those companies hold your data readable on their servers; here, no company ever does. The remaining distance to "theoretically maximal" is the self-hosted-behind-Tailscale end state, which your AI machine makes possible the day it arrives.

Rules that keep it this way

Never paste real account numbers or secrets into the repo or demo data · never configure a cloud LLM in the AI gateway if you want inference private · prefer CSV import over aggregators for accounts you consider most sensitive · check the sidebar says "Encrypted sync on" after setup.

7 · The AI machine day — bring the agents to life

  1. Install Ollama on the machine (ollama.com/download), then pull a model:
    ollama pull llama3.1:8b
  2. Serve it with browser access allowed (CORS), so HQ can reach it:
    OLLAMA_ORIGINS="*" ollama serve
    (Or LM Studio → Server tab → enable CORS. Anything with an OpenAI-compatible /v1 endpoint works.)
  3. Connect HQ: on a browser running on that machine or its network, open HQ → Settings → AI Gateway → preset "Ollama" → Test & save connection. The dot goes green; agents stop simulating; the Copilot wakes up. Every run stays on your hardware and is logged in the Agents screen.
  4. Install n8n for real communications (Gmail, Calendar, Shopify, Apple Health):
    docker run -d --name n8n -p 5678:5678 \
  -v n8n_data:/home/node/.n8n n8nio/n8n
    Authorize each account once inside n8n's UI, then set the bridge URL in HQ → Settings → n8n bridge. Ask Claude for the ready-made workflow templates when you get here.
  5. Install Tailscale on the machine and your phone — then HQ can reach your model and n8n securely from anywhere, and later you can host HQ itself there for the full self-hosted end state.

8 · Troubleshooting & FAQ

I lost my sync passphrase.

The cloud copy is unrecoverable — by design. Your data is still on any device that has it: export a backup there (Settings → Data), pick a new passphrase, enable sync fresh, import if needed.

Sync says "conflict."

Two devices edited while out of sync. HQ shows both timestamps and asks which copy wins — whole-copy, last-writer-wins. Pick the one with the work you'd hate to lose; the other side's recent edits are discarded.

The agents say "simulated."

Correct and honest — no local model is connected yet. They still assemble real summaries from your live data. Section 7 makes them real.

I opened the old URL and my data is gone.

It isn't — browser data is per-address. Your data lives at whichever URL you entered it on. Export there / import at hq.aarondweymann.workers.dev, or just enable sync on both with the same passphrase.

Can family members use it?

Anyone opening the URL gets their own blank copy — handing someone your sync passphrase hands them everything, so don't share it. True multi-user with per-workspace permissions is the trigger for the bigger backend build (Claude has the plan).

How do I start over?

Settings → Data → Reset to demo data (this device), and Delete cloud copy if you want the synced record gone too.
