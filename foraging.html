⚠️ Read this first

This app is an educational reference — not medical advice and not a substitute for expert identification.

  • Positive ID saves lives. Some plants listed here have look-alikes that can kill you (hemlock, deadly nightshade, foxglove, autumn crocus, yew, false hellebore). Never eat or use a plant unless a qualified local expert has confirmed it in person.
  • Try one new plant at a time, in a small amount first, to check for personal reactions or allergies.
  • Herbal medicine can interact with drugs and is not safe for everyone. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, children, and existing conditions all change what is safe. Consult a doctor or qualified herbalist.
  • Forage clean & legal. Avoid roadsides, sprayed land, and polluted water. Know local laws and protected species, and never over-harvest.
  • In an emergency (suspected poisoning), call your local poison control or emergency services immediately.

By continuing you acknowledge you use this information at your own risk.
